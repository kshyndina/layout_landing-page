What is FYSM?

FYSM is structured yoga that mixes static holds, controlled movement, breath and recovery points. You start from a version that makes sense for your body, then build from there.

Strength + range

Holds and controlled transitions work strength, mobility and stability together instead of treating yoga as stretching only.

Calm + focus

Breath gives the practice a rhythm and brings attention back into the body while you work.

Challenging, not mindless

Static and statodynamic work give you enough variation to stay switched on without repeating the same flow forever.

Built in layers

Simpler variations come first. More range, load or complexity gets added when you are ready for it.

FYSM is for you if...

you feel stiff and want to move better.
you want strength, not just stretching.
normal workouts bore you.
you want to feel more at home in your body.

Quick answers

Which level can I join?

It is beginner-friendly. Every pose can be adjusted to your current strength, mobility and experience, then built up from there.

Do I need to be flexible?

No. Flexibility is one of the things you can build here, alongside strength, control and body awareness.

Where can I practise?
