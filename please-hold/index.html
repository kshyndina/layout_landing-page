What is FYSM?

Structured yoga that mixes static holds, controlled movement, breath and recovery points. You start from a version that makes sense for your body, then build from there.

Strength + range

Holds and controlled transitions build strength, mobility and stability together, not stretching for stretching’s sake.

Calm + focus

Breath gives the work a rhythm and keeps your attention in your body while you move.

Challenging, not repetitive

Static and statodynamic modes keep the challenge changing without repeating the same flow forever.

Built in layers

Simpler variations come first. Range, load and complexity get added as you are ready for them.

FYSM is for you if...

you feel stiff and want to move better.
you want strength, not just stretching.
normal workouts bore you.
you want to feel more at home in your body.

Quick answers

Which level can I join?

It’s beginner-friendly. Every pose has a version that can meet you at your current strength, mobility and experience, and you build it out from there.

Do I need to be flexible?

No. Flexibility is one of the things you can build here, alongside strength, control and body awareness.

Where can I practise?

Da Nang + online. Check the schedule, join a Studio waitlist or ask me about 1:1.
