What is FYSM?

Structured yoga that mixes static holds, controlled movement, breath and recovery points. You start from a version that makes sense for your body, then build from there.

Strength + range

Holds and controlled transitions build strength, mobility and stability together, not stretching for stretching’s sake.

Calm + focus

Breath gives the work a rhythm and keeps your attention in your body while you move.

Challenging, not repetitive

Static and statodynamic modes keep the challenge changing without repeating the same flow forever.

Built in layers

Simpler variations come first. Range, load and complexity get added as you are ready for them.

Quick answers

Which level I have to have to join?

Beginner-friendly. Every pose has a simpler version for where your strength, mobility and experience are now, and you build from there.

Do I need to be flexible?

No. Flexibility is one of the things you can build here, alongside strength, control and body awareness.

Can yoga help with back pain or stiffness?

Yoga can be useful for some people with persistent low-back pain and stiffness, but this is not medical treatment. Tell me about pain or injuries before class, and get medical advice for new, severe or unexplained symptoms.

How often should a beginner practise yoga?

Two or three sessions a week is a practical starting rhythm. Consistency matters more than forcing a big schedule you will not keep.

What should I bring to my first yoga class?

Comfortable clothes, water and a mat if you have one. For My Khe Beach, bring a towel or something you do not mind putting under the mat.

Where can I practise yoga in Da Nang or online?

Free community practices are at My Khe Beach in Da Nang. Online practices run as YouTube livestreams, and private 1:1 sessions are online.
