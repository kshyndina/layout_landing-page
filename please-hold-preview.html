Questions people ask before they start.

Is this actually for beginners?

Yes. Please, Hold is primarily for beginners, people returning to movement and people who tried yoga before but did not feel at home in the format.

Do I need to be flexible?

No. Flexibility is part of what you can build. Strength, control and body awareness matter too.

Where do classes happen?

In Da Nang and online. Free public practices, Studio groups and private 1:1 options are shown through the current schedule or waitlist.

What is FYSM?

A structured yoga practice using preparation, activation, recovery and posture sequences. Breath counts and practice modes give the work a clear framework.

What if I have not exercised in years?

That is a normal starting point. Begin with a free practice or a private session if you want more individual attention.
